Monthly Offerings

Studio Twenty Six monthly offerings at 5303 Claremont Ave, Oakland. No experience required. Supplies included. Sliding scale by email.

  • Figure Drawing — Peace Love Draw. $25. Live model, music, charcoal and paper provided.
  • Costume Design — The Craft Show. $55. Bring a garment. Leave with something else.
  • Wake and Make — $40. Sunday morning collage. Coffee and tea available.
  • Art Church — $50. A live choir sings while the room makes.
  • Paint the Town — $55. One paint medium each month, plus a DJ.
  • Ride or Dye — $65. Dye and textile workshop. Different technique every month.

No street parking on Sundays — farmers market, and cars get towed. The lot across the street is available after 5pm on Thursdays. No refund within 24 hours of the session.
